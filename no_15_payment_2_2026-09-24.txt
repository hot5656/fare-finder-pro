  What was asked and answered:
  1. Can an admin choose between 使用綠界付款 and 不需付款? Not today. The only
     admin setting is v1_compare_enabled. Every subscription goes through ECPay,
     and the paywall is always on.
  2. Can a payment_required switch be added? Yes. The switch itself is small: a
     new allowed key in flight-admin-settings, a default row set to on, and a
     second switch on /admin. Most of the work is letting free subscriptions
     skip ECPay, which touches flight-subscribe, needs a payment_method = 'free'
     column, and affects current_period_end, cancel, the emails and the
     dashboard text.
  3. Can there be free mode with one-month subscriptions that expire 
     automatically and cancel immediately? Yes:
     - When payment is off, flight-subscribe sets the row to active directly
       with current_period_end = now() + 1 month.
     - Free rows expire with no grace period, so within about 30 minutes of the
       end date instead of 7 days (RENEWAL_GRACE_DAYS).
     - Cancelling a free row sets it to expired straight away and skips ECPay
       CreditCardPeriodAction.
     - The expired email wording needs a free-mode version.
     - The work is one migration, four Edge Functions (flight-subscribe,
       flight-cancel-subscription, flight-parser, flight-status-notification)
       and two front-end files.
  4. Decisions you made:
     - Free subscribers can re-subscribe as many times as they like, one month
       each time.
     - When the switch goes back to requiring payment, existing free
       subscriptions keep running until their own end date.
